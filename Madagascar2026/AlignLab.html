AlignLab DNA alignment viewer and editor · offline

  1. Load an aligned FASTA file (or the example).
  2. Scan the identity bar: dips mark variable sites, gaps and errors.
  3. Translate in frames 1–3: the right frame has no stop codons.
  4. Fix errors: click a base, then type, delete or add gaps.
  5. Export the edited alignment as FASTA.
