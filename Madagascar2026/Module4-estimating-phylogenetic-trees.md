## Bootstrapping 
When we estimate a tree, it is just a hypothesis of how things are related. Using the same data and analyzing again, we may get different results (hypotheses). Different genes for the same species may also give us a different hypothesis. Some of these relationships have stronger support than others, but we need a way to assess this!
>*How often do we recover the **same** tree from different analyses?*

One way to do this is by using bootstraps: a way of **resampling** our data
